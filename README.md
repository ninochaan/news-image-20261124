A newspaper front page that sums up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in a single
image — 44 stories: a New York jury convicts Huawei of bank fraud and
trade-secret theft, tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz (Iran's Guards
say) as Tehran rules out talks, Nvidia falls 7% as the Huang return rally
fades, a Houthi drone attack on a Riyadh tower injures 11, hostages are freed
as the Cartagena supermarket siege ends, an Israeli strike kills Hamas's
security chief in Gaza City, leaked data count 490,000 Russian war deaths,
the UK sanctions five Myanmar generals as 14 nations back the ICJ's Rohingya
ruling, the US measles toll reaches 14 dead, a M5.6 quake strikes Nepal,
Trump readies tariffs on 16 economies, Spain's Supreme Court amnesties
Puigdemont, the Senate counts finish 56–44, China probes Shanghai's party
chief, NATO sends an AWACS to Poland, and Sanofi buys Insmed in the biggest
pharma deal since 2023 — plus 28 numbered one-line briefs, a "WHERE IT
HAPPENED" world map, a big-numbers stat strip, and a DATE CHECK box naming
the big stories left out because they happened on another day.

Every headline is a verified 24 November 2026 event (sources in the build
workspace's STORY-POOL.md); one-party claims stay attributed. The image is
drawn deterministically by `gen.py` (Pillow, no network, no timestamps, no
randomness) and written to `entry.png` (1024×1024). `main.ts` copies
`entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
